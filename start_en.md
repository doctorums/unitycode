## Want your own node in the network?

Every UnityCode portal is a living node. There can be many of them, and all of them are part of one growing network. You can light up your own.

To “fork” means to make your own copy of this world. It becomes yours: change the texts, the colors, the sky, fill it with your own meaning. No one owns the network — everyone has their own voice in it.

### What you need

A free **GitHub** account — that’s where the portal code lives. If you don’t have one, signing up takes a minute.

### Three steps

- Press **Fork** — you’ll get your own copy of the portal.
- Change whatever you like: the name, the texts, the colors of the sky.
- Publish it — and your node will light up in the network.

Don’t be afraid to break things — this is your sandbox. If you want to dig deeper into what can be changed and what’s better left alone, open **“How to fork”**.

Welcome. The network is waiting for your frequency.
